**Note on the hashtag.** `#FollowTheMoney` is generic and carries no ideological or national tag. Avoid anything containing "laundering" or "fraud" in the caption or comments. The deck's own slide 8 says nobody has been charged, and a caption that implies otherwise would undercut the most defensible slide in the set.

**Note on slide 05 and screenshots.** Slide 05 is a fictional illustration. It carries its disclaimer twice in the pixels: a badge beside the kicker, which survives a top-crop, and a bordered note at the foot, which survives being read in full. Do not rely on the caption to carry that disclaimer. Carousel slides get screenshotted away from their captions, and this is the one slide in the series that would be actively misleading without it.
